Who I Am

Welcome! I'm Brittany Silva, a strategic communications leader, creative storyteller, and digital strategist based in Central Florida. With over a decade of experience spanning government, tourism, and community organizations, I specialize in helping teams and leaders turn complex ideas into compelling narratives that resonate.

Whether I'm guiding county officials through high-stakes media moments or designing integrated campaigns that spark public engagement, I bring a unique blend of clarity, creativity and data-driven insight to every project. My work is rooted in purpose: building trust, elevating brands, and connecting with the audiences that matter most.

What I Do Best

  • Strategic communication planning across digital and traditional platforms
  • Brand storytelling and visual direction that drives engagement
  • Crisis communication and executive media coaching
  • Marketing analytics and performance modeling
  • Public outreach and stakeholder coordination
  • Website strategy, SEO, and digital transformation

My Philosophy

I believe great communication is more than polished messaging — it's about creating meaningful connections. I thrive on collaboration, curiosity, and continuous learning, and I'm passionate about empowering organizations to lead with authenticity and impact.

Currently

I serve as a Communications Specialist for the Polk County Board of County Commissioners, where I lead brand consistency, multimedia strategy, and public engagement initiatives. I recently graduated with my Master's in Marketing, with specialization in Marketing Analytics from Western Governors University, which I use to deepen my expertise in data-informed decision-making.
